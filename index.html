Safety first

Safety and legal awareness

Fireworks are hazardous and can cause serious injury, fires and property damage. Safety and legal compliance must always take priority.

Follow official guidance

Observe all applicable local laws, restrictions and instructions issued by relevant authorities. Do not rely on this website as a substitute for official guidance.

Keep children away

Fireworks and related hazardous materials are not toys. Keep children and other unauthorised people away from them.

Do not handle unsafe items

Do not handle damaged, unidentified or malfunctioning fireworks. Leave hazardous situations to appropriately qualified emergency or safety professionals.

Emergency situations

If there is an immediate danger, move away and contact the appropriate local emergency service. Do not attempt to manage a hazardous incident yourself.
